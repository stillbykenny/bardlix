About

Producer. DJ. Idea machine.

I'm Bardlix – an electronic music producer and DJ. I build my own beats in FL Studio and shape the ideas together with AI tools to reach sounds I wouldn't have found alone.

I make a mix of styles, mostly Trance, Deep House and EDM – with vocals in both English and Swedish. It's all built for the dance floor: heavy basslines, big drops and atmospheres that carry you from the first track to the last.
